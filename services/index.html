Working together

Understand. Agree.
Make. Review.

  1. Share the current situation

    Your audience, existing website or tools, the problem and any timing or budget you have in mind.

  2. Agree the scope

    Define the useful outcome, deliverables, exclusions, ownership, review process and responsibilities.

  3. Review the actual work

    Check the website or workflow and the important visitor tasks before launch. Concepts and demonstrations stay clearly labelled.

  4. Plan the handover

    Document how the result is used, how enquiries arrive, which accounts you own and what maintenance is included.
