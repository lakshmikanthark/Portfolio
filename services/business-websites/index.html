I’m Lakshmikantha R K, a Bengaluru-based freelance website developer. I create business websites that explain your offer, work across screen sizes and give visitors a clear route to enquire. Bengaluru is also known as Bangalore; the project starts with your audience and the outcome you need.

01 / The right problem

Give people a reason
to keep going.

You may be starting with a new business, an outdated website or an offer that is difficult to explain online. The useful starting point is understanding what a visitor needs to know and what they should be able to do next.

I bring business understanding, design direction and AI-assisted website development into that process. We agree the scope around the actual problem.

02 / Possible scope

What we can
work through.

Message and structure
Clarify the audience, offer and page sequence so the website has a purpose beyond its opening impression.
Responsive website
Design and build a website with readable layouts, relevant content and useful navigation across screen sizes.
Enquiry journey
Make it clear how someone can contact you, request information or take the next appropriate step. Agree how enquiries are actually received.
Search foundations
Prepare descriptive titles, page descriptions, crawlable links and appropriate sitemap and structured information for the agreed public pages.
Launch and handover
Check the agreed visitor journey, explain the setup, and document how to update the website. Hosting and account ownership are discussed before launch.

These are scope options. The final pages, features, integrations and support are agreed before the project begins.

03 / The process

Understand. Make.
Review. Launch.

  1. Start with the brief

    Share your business, audience, current website if you have one, and what needs to change.

  2. Agree the direction

    Define the important pages, content, visitor actions and project boundaries before building.

  3. Build and review

    Review the actual website, test the important interactions and resolve the agreed changes.

  4. Prepare the handover

    Confirm the destination, account access and responsibilities, then document the agreed launch and update process.

Ownership and handover

Know what you own.
Know what comes next.

Before the build, we agree who owns the domain, hosting and source files, how enquiries are received, and what ongoing support includes. Accounts should stay under your control, with access shared for the agreed work.

Website launches, content updates and maintenance are different responsibilities. The quote and handover describe which are included. If enquiries need a tracker or follow-up workflow, explore the automation service and try a sample enquiry flow.

05 / Before we begin

A few practical
questions.

What does a website cost?

The quote depends on the pages, content, interactions and integrations you need. Share the brief and any budget range you have in mind so we can discuss a realistic scope.

How long does a project take?

We agree the timeline after understanding the scope, content readiness and review process. A specific delivery date is confirmed with the project brief.

What should I prepare?

A short explanation of the business, intended audience, services or products, existing brand assets, relevant images and examples of the information visitors often ask for. Read the launch checklist.

Can you improve an existing website?

Send the current URL and the issue you want to solve. I’ll review the setup before discussing whether a focused improvement or a larger change makes sense.
