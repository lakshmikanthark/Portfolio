01 / Approach

From understanding
to making.

I start with the problem: who needs help, what they are trying to do, and where the current experience gets in their way. That thinking shapes the website, the workflow and the next step a person should be able to take.

My work includes process mapping, requirements, stakeholder coordination, digital commerce and AI-assisted website development. I use AI as part of the building process, alongside planning, reviewing and testing the result.

LK and LKRK refer to Lakshmikantha R K on this website. Lakshmikantha RK is the same name written without the space before the initials. Similar spellings such as Lakshmikanth or Lakshmi Kanth can refer to other people; use my full name and Bengaluru, or this domain, to identify my work.

What can I work with you on?

Freelance websites, interaction design, AI workflows, and consistent design or content for a business. Share the problem, your current setup and the outcome you want; we can then discuss the scope. Explore business solutions or read about practical automation.

Are you open to professional roles?

Yes. I’m open to Business Analyst and Business Operations opportunities, alongside relevant freelance projects. My résumé and project work provide more context.
