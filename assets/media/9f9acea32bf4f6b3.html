Before you start

The useful
details.

Does it capture my chats automatically?

No. You paste text, import a supported conversation file, or select local files yourself. The extension does not scrape your open AI chats, connect to provider accounts, or send messages on your behalf.

Can I use it with different AI tools?

You can copy the prepared text into an AI tool that accepts pasted text. You decide where it goes. Each provider has its own input limits and policies, and original files need to be attached separately.

What does Smart mode do?

Smart uses local rules to select high-signal turns and focus keywords. It is not an AI-generated summary. It can omit or clip content, and shows that tradeoff so you can review it. Full and Split preserve the imported conversation text.

How do I install the extension?

This release is a private pilot for desktop Chrome. Download the ZIP, extract it, and use Load unpacked in Chrome’s extensions page. No Chrome Web Store listing or approval is claimed. Follow the Windows installation guide.

How does payment and activation work?

When annual access opens, sign in, pay the displayed amount to the displayed UPI payee, and submit your transaction reference. Access starts after the payment is confirmed against the bank record. Your account then shows the licence key. A reference alone does not activate access.

What happens after 365 days?

There is no automatic charge. You can choose to renew. Existing capsules remain readable, exportable and deletable. Creating or updating capsules beyond the free allowance needs active annual access.

Can I recover a forgotten vault password?

The vault password protects locally encrypted data. We cannot recover that password or decrypt the vault for you. Keep a backup and your password somewhere safe. Your website account password is separate.

Can I really transfer from ChatGPT to Claude or Gemini?

Yes, by manually copying or importing conversation content you are allowed to reuse. Briefspindle prepares a portable text handoff. It does not link accounts, guarantee a full platform export, move file bytes automatically, or send anything for you. Read the transfer guide.

Is there a recurring subscription or a no-refund rule?

No automatic subscription is billed: annual access lasts 365 days from verified activation, and you choose whether to renew. Read our refund and remedy policy; payment defects and consumer rights are never waived by a blanket no-refund rule.

How do I ask for a demo or help?

Use the published support contact once live, or read our help page. Do not send private conversations, vault passwords, PINs or banking credentials to support.
